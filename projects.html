Quantitative retrieval and spatiotemporal analysis of soil quality parameters using geospatial datasets and machine learning techniques in Western Plains and Ghat regions of Karnataka
  • Worked on multi-sensor integration for soil quality retrieval, focusing on Soil Electrical Conductivity (EC) and Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) modelling.
  • Developed a data fusion model combining in-situ soil measurements, remote sensing data, topographic variables, and hydrometeorological parameters.
  • Mapped soil organic carbon using Sentinel-1 SAR data and automated processing workflows in SNAP.

Evaluation of water quality retrieval equations using multispectral remote sensing imageries in Bhavanisagar Reservoir
  • Monitored optically active water quality parameters in Bhavanisagar Reservoir using in-situ measurements.
  • Compared retrieval performance from Landsat-9 OLI-2 and Sentinel-2 MSI data.

  • Identified Sentinel-2 as the stronger option for higher-spatial-resolution water quality assessment.
