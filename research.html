Research Interests

The questions and methods at the centre of my work — spanning hydrology, remote sensing, and geospatial modelling.

Hydrology and Climate Interactions
Studying rainfall-temperature relationships and their connections with land cover and catchment-scale hydrological response.
Satellite Remote Sensing
Using optical and SAR satellite datasets for environmental monitoring, soil studies, and water resources applications.
Water Quality Modelling
Retrieving and modelling water quality parameters from in-situ measurements and multispectral satellite observations.
Geospatial Machine Learning
Developing data fusion workflows that combine field observations, terrain variables, hydrometeorological data, and remote sensing products.
